Project Overview

This project compares two Reddit corpora, one from James Madison University (JMU) and one from the University of Virginia (UVA), to understand how each student community discusses place and expresses sentiment toward different types of locations. By examining spatial patterns and sentiment trends across both datasets, we aimed to uncover how students at the two institutions talk about place and whether they engage with different types of locations in their conversations.

Our project is guided by the hypothesis that JMU students primarily discuss local, on-campus spaces with a positive sentiment, while UVA students reference global or politically significant places, particularly in the Middle East, more often and express a varied sentiment about them.
