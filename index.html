Project Overview

Over the past several weeks, we have been researching how students at JMU and other colleges Virginia feel about their campus and the surrounding world based on information posted on Reddit. This research has been highly technical and has required thorough vetting of the data. It was not designed for a larger audience. For the final assignment, you are going to translate complex data to a general audience by telling a data-driven story through a “storymap”. This is a dynamic map that tells a spatial story by moving the viewport from location to location. The key to a successful storymap is balancing intellectual depth with accessibility. You should tell a good story, but it also should not be too long.
